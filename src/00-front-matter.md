## About the Author

**Horace Chan** is a lifelong engineer who thinks in code but dreams in symbols. After years spent working with computers, networks, and algorithms, he became fascinated by how the same numerical languages that power software—binary, hex, timestamps, hashes—could be used as a mirror for the human psyche.

*Base 16 Numerology* grew out of that obsession: a project to fuse Unix time, ASCII, and open‑source–style thinking with the ancient urge to find meaning in numbers. Horace writes for readers who are just mystical enough to enjoy a good archetype, and just skeptical enough to want to see the math underneath it.

When he is not writing about hex digits and soul codes, Horace consults with clients around the world, helping them decode their personal Hex Soul Maps and navigate life transitions using the language of Base 16. His approach blends the rigor of a systems engineer with the intuition of someone who has spent decades listening to the quiet patterns inside numbers.

## A Note to the Reader

You hold in your hands a book that asks you to suspend two kinds of disbelief at once.

First, if you are a spiritually inclined reader, you may need to accept that the symbols in this book come from engineering—not from ancient scrolls, crystal bowls, or channeled entities. The language is hex, the calendar is Unix time, and the hash function is documented in plain code. There is no hidden guru. The algorithm is the guru, and you can read its source.

Second, if you are a technically minded reader, you may need to accept that numbers can serve as mirrors—that a deterministic hash applied to your name can produce a result that *feels* meaningful, not because of magic, but because structured self-reflection is powerful regardless of its origin.

Both kinds of readers are welcome. In fact, both are necessary. Base 16 Numerology lives in the gap between myth and machine, and it needs interpreters from both worlds.

This book will teach you:

- **The system:** how names and birth moments become hex digits through Unicode, Unix timestamps, and the LIBRE-256 hash.
- **The archetypes:** what each of the sixteen hex digits (0 through f) means as a symbolic pattern.
- **The map:** how to arrange your personal hex digits into a Hex Soul Map and read it as a story about your life.
- **The cycles:** how to track Personal Hex Years and use them as a timing tool for decisions, reflection, and growth.
- **The practice:** how to apply hex numerology to relationships, career, places, and daily life.

You will not need to do math by hand. The companion website handles all conversions. Your job is the harder one: to sit with the results and ask whether they describe something real about you.
